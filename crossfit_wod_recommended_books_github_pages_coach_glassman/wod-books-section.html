5 books recommended by Coach Glassman in early Workout of the Day posts

01

Protein Power

Michael R. Eades and Mary Dan Eades

Workout of the Day - 12 February 2001

The Eades’ book Protein Power is a classic in responsible nutrition. Publishers of best selling books are typically not interested in publishing the author’s bibliography. Here are the foundations for Protein Power. Next time someone tells you that reduced carbohydrate diets are fads with no scientific basis ask them if they’ve read any of the hundreds of scholarly works cited by Michael and Mary Dan Eades.
Buy on Amazon

02

The Anatomy Coloring Book

Wynn Kapit and Lawrence M. Elson

Workout of the Day - 28 March 2001

Picking up The Anatomy Coloring Book and following its fun prescription for learning anatomy through coloring in the anatomical charts provides a major advance to your understanding of your body and how it functions. Get the book and fine tip felt markers in no fewer than twenty colors, and color a couple of plates a week. Within a year you’ll have seriously advanced your understanding of the human body. Read the instructions; that’s essential.
Buy on Amazon

03

Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning

National Strength and Conditioning Association

Workout of the Day - 5 April 2001

We routinely get requests for basic information on exercise science. If you own only one reference on exercise it ought to be the National Strength and Conditioning Association’s Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning. The NSCA is a non-profit agency that publishes several peer-reviewed journals of exercise science. They have no peer in the field of strength and conditioning research.
Buy on Amazon

04

Endurance: Shackleton’s Incredible Voyage

Alfred Lansing

Workout of the Day - 11 April 2001

As athletes we endeavor to show courage and determination. Endurance: Shackleton’s Incredible Voyage may be the greatest story (true!) of perseverance ever told. We encourage all our athletes to read it.
Buy on Amazon

05

The Weightlifting Encyclopedia: A Guide to World Class Performance

Arthur J. Drechsler

Workout of the Day - 14 April 2001

Check out the Encyclopedia of Weightlifting. This is the standard reference to the sport of weightlifting. Every coach and athlete who uses the Clean and Jerk and the Snatch needs this book. The Weightlifting Encyclopedia.

Workout of the Day - 20 July 2001

We’ve once before recommended the Weight Lifting Encyclopedia. This gem should be in every coach and athlete’s library. This link is an excellent starting point to consider Olympic Lifting.
